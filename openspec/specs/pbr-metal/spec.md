# pbr-metal Specification

## Purpose
Metal and environment reflections on toon materials marked `_PBR`/`_SIMPLE_PBR`: how metal and roughness are read from the material parameters and the mask, the generated environment, matcap, and the cost. (Purpose TBD, synced from change add-pbr-metal.)
## Requirements
### Requirement: Reflections from the PBR parameters
With toon shading on, the viewer SHALL give every toon material marked `_PBR` or `_SIMPLE_PBR` a reflection of the environment,
and a direct highlight from the key light where `_PBRSpecularIntensity` is above 0 (`_PBR` only). How metallic and how rough each
texel is SHALL come from `_Metallic`, `_Roughness`, their map scales and, for `_PBR`, the `_PBRMask`; metal SHALL tint its
reflection with its own colour and lose its diffuse colour by its metallic value; the reflection's strength SHALL follow
`_CubemapIntensity`, `_CubemapColor` and `_PBRRate`. The cel bands, outlines, self-shadow and tone mapping SHALL stay as they are.
Materials without these keywords SHALL get no reflection. No value SHALL be hard-coded per skin.

#### Scenario: Boots
- **WHEN** Generic - Final is shown with toon shading, boots in view
- **THEN** the white enamel shows soft streaks of light and grey along its shape and the silver trims show bright and dark bands, as in the huaxu reference, instead of flat paint

#### Scenario: Rough versus polished
- **WHEN** Night Sonata is shown with toon shading
- **THEN** a part with roughness near 0 (`Alpha02EX`) reflects sharply and a part with roughness near 1 (`Alpha05`) only shows a soft sheen

#### Scenario: Not on other materials
- **WHEN** the face, skin or hair is shown with toon shading
- **THEN** it has no reflection

### Requirement: Mask reading decided and recorded
The `_PBRMask` SHALL be read with G as metal and R as roughness (H10), and how the mask combines with `_Metallic`, `_Roughness` and their map
scales SHALL be decided by comparing the current product/sum reading with a reading in which the mask decides (scalars only where there is no
mask) on all four frames: Stigmata's `Cloth` (`_Metallic` 0, `_Roughness` 1), Crepuscule's Generic boots and crown, and Night Sonata (map scales
0.5 to 1.1). One reading SHALL be kept for every material; it SHALL leave Crepuscule's boots and crown as they look after `add-pbr-metal`. The
toon highlight on `_PBR` materials SHALL be masked by smoothness under the kept reading. The decision SHALL be recorded with the material facts.

#### Scenario: Trims are polished
- **WHEN** the Generic boots' trims are shown
- **THEN** they read as polished silver, not as matte grey

#### Scenario: Armour pieces on another frame
- **WHEN** Stigmata Generic - Final is shown with toon shading
- **THEN** the arm and leg guards that its `_PBRMask` marks as polished metal read as dark polished metal, not as rough grey cloth

### Requirement: Generated environment
The environment reflected SHALL be generated by the viewer from three.js's own `RoomEnvironment` and prefiltered for roughness,
built at most once per viewer when first needed, shared by all toon materials, and freed when the viewer is disposed. It SHALL not
be loaded from files or from any remote host, and SHALL not change the original materials.

#### Scenario: One environment
- **WHEN** all seven skins are selected one after another with toon shading on
- **THEN** one environment texture exists the whole time, and the texture count per skin is the same on a second pass

#### Scenario: Original materials unchanged
- **WHEN** toon shading is switched off
- **THEN** the GLB's own materials show without reflections, as before this change

### Requirement: Matcap
Toon materials marked `_USE_MATCAP` SHALL add their `_MatCapTex`, looked up by the view-space normal and scaled by `_MatcapMask`
and the metal mask, if this reads like the reference on the materials where it covers large areas (Stigmata's and Veritas Grace's `Body`);
otherwise matcap SHALL stay off and the decision SHALL be recorded.

#### Scenario: Large matcap surfaces
- **WHEN** Stigmata Generic - Final and Veritas Grace are shown with toon shading, upper body in view
- **THEN** their `Body` either shows the matcap sheen like the reference or matches the build without matcap, as recorded

### Requirement: Cost of reflections
The added GPU memory, shader programs and CPU time per frame SHALL be measured per skin and recorded; the environment SHALL be
counted once.

#### Scenario: Measured
- **WHEN** the change is complete
- **THEN** the material facts list the environment's size, the program count per skin and the CPU per frame with and without reflections

### Requirement: Dark metal stays dark
The environment reflection and the tone mapping exposure SHALL keep dark metallic materials dark: a material whose albedo is dark SHALL NOT
read lighter than its surroundings in the reference because of the reflected environment. If the generated environment or the exposure has
to change for this, Crepuscule's in-game comparisons SHALL still hold, and the new values SHALL be recorded.

#### Scenario: Ink-lit Hermit
- **WHEN** Veritas' Ink-lit Hermit is shown with toon shading at the default light
- **THEN** its suit reads near-black teal with highlights, as on huaxu, not mid grey
